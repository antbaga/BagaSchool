# Planet textures

Source: Solar System Scope (https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), by INOVE,
based on NASA elevation and imagery data.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: downscaled to 1024 x 512 (Earth: 2048 x 1024) and re-encoded as JPEG for
offline use. Venus uses the atmosphere (cloud) map. The Saturn ring strip
is used as-is (512 px wide) and turned into a ring disc at runtime.

Files were taken from the 2K pack as mirrored in
github.com/Wartets/Relativity-Simulator (assets/textures/solarsystemscope).
